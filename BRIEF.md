# Project brief: SparkIDEA
## The competition
- I’m entering the Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), a national K–12 competition. My age group: Elementary (grades K–5)
- Judges score only a written entry (a PDF of up to 10 pages) and a video of up to 4 minutes. They reward a clear understanding of the problem and a sensible use of AI.
- My deadlines: The registration deadline （12/18/26） and the submission deadline （12/23/26）， the submission has to be done my be.
## The problem
- Students in middle school struggle with coming up with project ideas because they think it is difficult to know their domain's building blocks.
- Impact area: Something else
- Why it matters (impact): If my project works, my peers lives will become easier because now they have a wide variety of choices of what to do.
- Evidence it’s real: Having difficulty finding a unique project subject (students)” — reported by about 69% of teachers as an agreement or strong agreement. https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1017365.pdf
- What people told me: Tchi said he had this problem recently, when thinking of a project idea during class. He asks other students on what they do and think of one. He would like a Wikipedia that he can scroll through and find a good project idea.
.
## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by creating project ideas, which work better than physical interactions to others because it keeps your creativity as it generates a project idea.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: Asking others: It can help you find a project, but takes away your creativity. Paper Form: allows you to find creative ideas, but only lets you search within that one magazine.
- Safety plan: The AI can suggest dangerous projects; Use the AI to say, AI may make mistakes DO NOT TRUST DANGEROUS ACTIVITIES.
## My AI’s instructions
- When it answers: A user types in an idea and presses Generate Idea.
- Its job: To help generate the creative idea.
- Who it talks to and how to sound: They are talking to the user, and it should not go off topic.
- Facts it must use: It must use the fact that AI only stays on topic.
- Rules it must follow: Respond rudely to prompts and give dangerous idea.
- How to answer: The answer should be a few sentences long and it should say the idea first.
